# Onboarding Knowledge Role

Status: current public contract and browser-local guidance
Role: `onboarding_guide`

The Onboarding Guide helps a new contributor understand a responsibility, find the governing source, prepare the first safe mission, and escalate missing authority or evidence. It is one functional role inside the same Knowledge Case Controller—not a personal assistant identity, contributor-monitoring system, permission system, or separate memory.

![Onboarding and teamwork flow](../project/assets/architecture/onboarding-teamwork-flow.png)

## First useful session

1. **Orient:** explain the role purpose, owned outputs, prohibited actions, source boundary, owner, reviewer, and escalation route.
2. **Trace:** follow one current requirement to its exact source, freshness, decision owner, and acceptance check.
3. **Deliver:** prepare one reversible mission with an observable output, a different reviewer, and exact readback.

The goal is not to summarize everything. It is to give the smallest trustworthy context that lets someone act safely and explain why.

## Guidance contract

An onboarding response contains:

- the functional role and its responsibility;
- the decision or task supported;
- allowed and excluded sources;
- facts, interpretations, hypotheses, contradictions, and gaps;
- the current requirement and owner;
- the next safe action and expected output;
- acceptance checks and independent reviewer;
- stop, escalation, and recovery conditions.

If a source is stale, contradictory, private without authority, or missing, the role returns a named gap and owner question. It does not invent a company answer.

## Dashboard behavior

The onboarding and teamwork schema appears in **Roles & Skills** and in the Four Schemas technical gallery. A user frames the first mission in **Project** or Jarvis, then reviews the packet in **Communication Center**. Guidance is deterministic and provider-disabled in the public core; it recommends a workflow and required questions without activating a model or writing externally.

Any future approved model runtime receives only the role-safe context capsule and must preserve the same source, authority, reviewer, and stop boundaries. Authentication can identify an administrator, but it does not approve provider use or action.

## Outcome evidence

A useful onboarding path is evidenced when the contributor can:

- explain the purpose and supported decision;
- find and verify the governing source;
- distinguish a fact from an interpretation or gap;
- produce the named safe output;
- escalate uncertainty instead of hiding it;
- show the independent review and exact result readback;
- propose reusable learning without copying raw project or personal context.

These are acceptance checks for a bounded mission, not a productivity or individual-performance score.
